## The CEF survival estimate (bounded, not backtested)

A precise backtest is blocked by the delisted-price wall (resolved funds' market
prices aren't free; yfinance purges them). Bounding from the *measured*
resolution rates × an assumed ~10% entry discount − 1% friction over a ~2yr hold:

- Central case (tilted ~60%, ~10% discount): **~2.5%/yr** discount-capture alpha
  *over the asset-class return*.
- Plausible range: **+0.7% to +3.6%/yr**.
- Character: slow (~2yr holds), convex (~half the basket does nothing),
  capacity-limited, poor capital efficiency. Real but modest — never
  life-changing.
